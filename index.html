JBE USA Pop-Up Tour

Masterclass & Workshop Objectives

The JBE USA Pop-Up Tour is a curated, hands-on experience designed around expert-led masterclasses and interactive workshops. Each session is delivered by strategic partners across real estate, healthcare, relocation, wellness, and medical tourism, offering practical insights, market guidance, and real partnership opportunities for participants looking to engage, invest, or transition into the Kenya ecosystem.

Overall Objective

To provide participants with practical, expert-led guidance through focused masterclasses and interactive workshops that equip them with the knowledge, tools, and connections needed to successfully engage, invest, relocate, or partner within the Kenya ecosystem.

Specific Objectives

1 Demystify the Kenya Market

Break down real estate, healthcare, relocation, wellness, and medical tourism pathways through clear, step-by-step masterclasses led by industry practitioners.

2 Enable Informed Decision-Making

Equip participants with practical frameworks, regulatory insights, cost structures, and risk considerations to support confident investment and transition decisions.

3 Provide Direct Access to Experts

Create structured, interactive sessions where participants can engage directly with vetted partners, ask questions, and receive real-time guidance.

4 Facilitate Practical Application

Use workshops to help participants map personalized action plans—whether for property investment, relocation planning, healthcare access, or wellness solutions.

5 Catalyze Strategic Partnerships

Connect participants with trusted service providers and partners to explore collaborations, service uptake, and long-term engagement beyond the tour.

6 Support Cross-Border Transition Readiness

Prepare participants for cultural, legal, financial, and lifestyle considerations involved in transitioning or doing business within the Kenyan ecosystem.
